METHODOLOGY
Bitails mode first fetches block summaries and prunes only blocks whose total output value is below the threshold; in qualifying blocks, output sums are fetched 100 transactions per request, transactions whose sum is below the threshold are ruled out, and the rest are downloaded raw, hash-checked against their TXID and parsed. WhatsOnChain mode binary-searches the date range and fetches transactions in batches of 20, without value pruning. Both are constrained by your API request budget.
Same-key outputs. Each standard (P2PKH) input reveals its public key; an output paying the hash of one of those keys goes back to the sender and is marked as likely change. Leaving sender is every other non-data output. Inputs of other types cannot be identified, and such rows are flagged.
⚠ Output to another address is not proof of a payment to another owner, and a same-key output is not proof of change: a sender can pay its own address on purpose, and change can go to a fresh address. Uninspected blocks/transactions remain explicitly unverified.
