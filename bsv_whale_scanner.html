METHODOLOGY
Bitails mode first fetches block summaries and prunes only blocks whose total output value is below the threshold; in qualifying blocks, transactions whose output sum is below the threshold are ruled out, and the rest are downloaded raw, hash-checked against their TXID and parsed. WhatsOnChain mode binary-searches the date range and fetches transactions in batches of 20, without value pruning. Both are constrained by your API request budget.
⚠ Output to another address is not proof of a payment to another owner. Change outputs, custody reshuffling and self-transfers can dominate whale rankings. Uninspected blocks/transactions remain explicitly unverified.
